What does this Pi extension actually do? 这个 Pi 插件到底做什么？
It lets the Agent choose how many complete messages a reply needs. 它让 Agent 自己判断一次回复该用几条完整消息。
Every send_message call becomes one real chat bubble. 每次 send_message 调用，都会变成一个真实气泡。
Short answers stay short. Complex answers get room to breathe. 短回答保持简短，复杂回答自然分成几步。
