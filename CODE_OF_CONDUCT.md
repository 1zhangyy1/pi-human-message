# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive.

We welcome people of different backgrounds and experience levels. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable in project spaces.

Assume good intent, but focus discussion on observable behavior and evidence. Critique code, prompts, tests, and product decisions rather than people. Remove private user data from transcripts before sharing them.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile.
